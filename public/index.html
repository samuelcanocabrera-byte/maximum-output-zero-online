HOW THE FIGHT WORKS

Strike combos: tap Attack to chain a 5-hit string: jab, cross, hook, front kick, then finisher. Up + Attack launches; Down + Attack sweeps low. Different characters lean into that string differently, so Jin stays measured, Arata presses forward aggressively, and Shirogane snaps through faster motions. Defense: hold Guard to reduce damage; press just before a hit for a Just Guard. Dash through an enemy attack to evade and charge Awakening.

RCT: press Heal with enough CE to restore health. Hold Down + Heal instead to perform your character's Cursed Technique Reversal (CTR). Techniques spend CE (blue meter), and the yellow Awakening meter fills through combat.

NEW: INTERACTIVE DOMAIN CLASH

A brief hand-sign cinematic plays for each character. Spend full 100 Awakening + 35 CE to start a Domain. If you lack either resource, the attempt does nothing. The other player has a 2.7-second counter window: press their Domain key with at least 35 Awakening + 28 CE to challenge it. Insufficient resources make the challenge fail safely, without spending either meter. During the 12-second barrier fight, move around the arena and land real punches, kicks, or Techniques. Each successful hit deals virtual barrier damage instead of reducing actual HP. Defend, dash, and combo like a normal fight. The player with the most damage dealt wins the Domain. Third player: press Domain within the first 3.2 seconds to join. Real HP stays frozen until the clash ends. Ties shatter the Domains!

♠ KISARAGI JIN HEAVENLY AXIOMS

Q / U / 7: cycle the eleven visible card choices, or click the wheel to select; hold Dash + Cycle to reverse. Technique: use selected card. Spades cut, Diamonds grant armor, Clubs store/release energy, Hearts tether targets, Aces have exceptional power, and Joker is a permanent-use 50/50 wager (two per fight). Guard + Technique: cast the selected card on yourself: Spades sharpen your fists, Diamonds reinforce your body, Clubs store incoming force, Hearts anchor you, Joker wagers to restore yourself. Down + Guard + Technique: Providence Siphon. Down + RCT: Inverse Decree. Maximum: Royal Flush. Domain: Fated Misfortune: All violence becomes impossible. Jin declares each of up to five perfectly matching stakes and confirms it with Attack. Every living fighter must possess the stake. Select with Left / Right (or Cycle) or click; Attack confirms. The roulette is immutably 90% house / 10% challengers; neither Providence Siphon nor skills can influence it. Only the losing side forfeits its own stake. The house never steals the value. After five spins, the Domain closes.

➜ ARATA MICHIZANE MOMENTUM

Momentum 0–200%: successful hits and perfectly evaded strikes gain 10%; taking a hit loses 5%; standing still too long starts decay. Arata fights best while constantly advancing. Technique: Avalanche consumes Momentum for a long-range burst. Up + Technique: stronger Avalanche (greater cost). Hold Guard + Technique: Resistance dampens attacks while draining Momentum. Down + RCT: Inertia Reversal. Maximum: Overdrive temporarily reaches 300% power. OVER OVERDRIVE: rapidly tap Down three times (S / ↓ / 5) with 85 CE to enter a stronger 6-second state. Once per match; causes 10-second burnout afterward. Domain: Endless Horizon: Arata points to the sky. A rainy forest and icy mountains appear. His Momentum instantly reaches 200% and stays frozen for the entire Domain, while opponents suffer escalating confusion, slowed movement and inevitable clarity pulses.

◇ SHIROGANE AIZAWA QUANTUM FLICKER

Cycle: create 10 solid-light holograms; they distract foes and absorb hits. Technique: Luminal Lash fires prismatic beams. Down + Technique: Chronal Lag teleports through space and briefly evades attacks. Guard + Technique: Moment Capture first saves a snapshot, then rewinds to it on the next use. Down + RCT: Negative Exposure. Maximum: Overclock delivers a devastating light assault, then burns out his abilities. Domain: Kaleidoscope of Total Reflection: A blinding crystalline prism erases shadows. Enemy awareness is distorted, and Luminal Lash bounces across every facet: environmental sure-hit rays cannot be dodged or blocked by ordinary guarding.

SIMPLE DOMAIN: Every fighter holds Guard and presses Domain to create a small defensive barrier for 3.3 seconds. Costs 24 CE with a 9.5-second cooldown. Cancels sure-hit attacks but does not break Jin's no-violence wager rules or automatically win Domain clashes.
JIN SELF-CARDS: hold Guard + Technique to put the currently chosen card on Jin himself; Down + Guard + Technique activates Providence Siphon. ARATA OVER OVERDRIVE: tap Down three times within 1.05 seconds when CE is at least 85. Lasts 6 seconds, once per match, with a 10-second burnout. DOMAIN CLASH: 12 seconds of real movement and strikes, virtual damage only, largest total dealt wins. All actual HP stays frozen. JIN DOMAIN WAGERS: Once the rules appear, Jin chooses a stake with his Left / Right or Cycle key, or clicks one of five wager cards in the arena. Attack or Domain confirms. The wheel spins for each bet. Nonviolence is absolute, including projectiles already on screen. The bet is automatically mirrored on all living players in 2P or 3P. The result is 90% House / 10% challengers, independent of luck. Five spins closes the Domain. ARATA: impossible-to-dodge confusion pulses, Momentum locked at 200%. SHIROGANE: unavoidable prism rays and reflected Luminal Lash attacks.
