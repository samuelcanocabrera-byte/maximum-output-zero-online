MAXIMUM OUTPUT: ZERO - UPDATE PACKAGE (V8.2)
============================================

WHAT TO RUN
-----------
This whole folder is the game. Start it from THIS folder (the one that
contains package.json and server.mjs):

    npm start        ->  http://localhost:3000

You can also double-click public/index.html for local play.

The folder Maximum_Output_Zero_Deploy_Ready/ is an OLD copy of the game
from an earlier version. It does NOT contain these updates. Ignore it, or
delete it so nobody launches it by mistake.

WHAT IS NEW IN THIS VERSION
---------------------------
1. Attacking and blocking together behave correctly. An attack commits the
   fighter (guard frames no longer stack on top of a swing), and holding
   Guard blocks again the moment the attack recovers.

2. Shirogane rebalanced:
   - 265 HP (was 280), speed 314 (was 324), 8.0 regen (was 8.9)
   - Holograms: 6 clones x 2 hits, 27 CE, 4.2s, 30% absorb
     (was 10 x 3, 22 CE, 5.4s, 43%)
   - Luminal Lash: 13 damage per beam, 24 CE, 0.62s cooldown
   - Chronal Lag: 15 CE, shorter evade window, longer cooldown
   - Moment Capture: 30 CE, 0.95s cooldown, heal capped at 40% max HP,
     no longer refunds Cursed Energy
   - Overclock: 66 damage (was 86), 5.2s burnout (was 3.3s)
   - Domain rays and Negative Exposure reduced

3. Hido's constructs are drawn as real weapons: crystal sword (blade,
   crossguard, hilt), spear (shaft and leaf blade), faceted shield, and a
   linked chain. Same damage and hitboxes as before.

4. Online 3-player rooms work. The lobby has a 2 PLAYERS / 3 PLAYERS
   selector, and the room's size now drives input routing and match start.

5. Typing fixed. The game no longer swallows letters in text fields, so
   room codes and player names register every keystroke.

6. Custom keybinds. Click KEYS in the top bar, click a key, press a new
   one. Conflicting keys swap. RESTORE DEFAULTS resets everything. The
   layout is saved in the browser and used in online rooms too.

7. Controller support for PS4/PS5, Xbox and Switch Pro (standard mapping):
   stick/D-pad move, A/Cross jump, X/Square strike, Y/Triangle technique,
   B/Circle guard, LB/L1 dash, RB/R1 cycle, LT/L2 RCT, RT/R2 Domain,
   R3 Maximum, Options/Menu emote. Keyboard and controller work together.

8. Emotes. Press T (P1), ; (P2) or Numpad + (P3), or Options on a
   controller, to open the palette; Left/Right selects; press again to
   send. Other players see the emote online.

9. Credits. CREDITS in the menu footer: Samuel, Ateeb, Wendao.
